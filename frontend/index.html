❓ How to play

WASD/arrows walk · mouse aims · attack button/click/space uses your default weapon · 1-9 hotbar (spells + items — pressing a weapon key makes it the default) · Q uses your off-hand slot (spells/throwables fire; weapons/shields/empty parry) · J quests · E talk/loot/descend · M/tap minimap: map · P party (ride mounts from the beasts tab) · I inventory · C character · shift run · Esc menu (save/load, sound, title)

🔍 The world renders at one fixed scale — zoom with your browser instead (Ctrl/⌘ + +/-, Ctrl+wheel, or pinch). ⌨️ Press F11 to enter Fullscreen: it fills the screen without the browser hijacking the Esc key (Esc opens the menu).

👤 Your panel lives top-right: portrait, level/XP, points, HP, mana, gold — click it (or V) for the full profile, click the points row (or C) to spend.

📜 Work boards itself: villagers offer quests when you talk (or ask with 📜 Quest / "got work?") — fetch caches glow gold, slay targets get ❗ beacons, delves mark themselves. Track everything in 📜 Quests (J): objectives, distances, rewards, report-backs. Bosses and legendary relics issue their own calls.

🤚 Your off-hand guards as well as it hurls: pick any hotbar slot as your off-hand in 🎒 Inventory, then press Q (or the 🤚 button) to use it — spells, torches and throwables fire as normal, but a weapon, a shield, or an empty slot parries instead (off-hand steel never swings). A timed parry cuts the blow to 1/5 bare-handed, 1/10 with a weapon, 1/20 × shield tier with a shield (mythril takes only 0.015). Shields also guard passively (+DEF/+SDEF by tier), and the 🤺 Parrying + 🛡️ Shieldwork skill trees widen the window, teach auto-parries and rebounds — parry your way to the 🛡️ Stalwart Reflector technique, which mirrors every attack for 2s.

👤 header ▾/▸ collapses the hero panel (it starts expanded); drag the minimap's ⤡ corner to resize it. Top-left order is ☰ menu, 💾 save, 🔔 notifications, 📜 quests, 🛠️ practice setup (practice worlds only), ❓ help, then the 📍 location box — click the location box (or the minimap, or M) for the full map. Loot from chests and corpses also lands in a card under the minimap for a few seconds.

🔔 Notices scroll past as a single line at the very top — nothing stacks up. The 🔔 button (upper-left, or N) opens the full notification history.

🌍 The AI dreams each region as you enter it: names, lore, NPCs, rumors — all saved by coordinates. Villages, towns, cities, canal watergates, sakura towns and dwarven fortresses hide out in the wilds — follow the map's gold ⌂ ♛ 🌊 🌸 ⛰ hearths (each labelled 🐴 on the map). Purple 🕳️ tiles are dungeon entrances. On the map: drag to pan, wheel to zoom.

🐴 Every hearth keeps one Stable beast lodge — look for the floating 🐴 over its hay pen, or follow the party panel's Find Stable → compass. Press E at the pen nexus to store, retrieve, ride or release tamed beasts (stabled beasts mend even from 0 HP). Guards, hunters and travelers may ride their own mounts — a 🐴 in their nameplate.

🗺️ Danger has an address: towns are safe (~lv3), deep forests ~10, deserts ~20, the seven highlands (mountains, volcanoes, frostcrown glaciers, blightmire bogs, stormcrag cliffs, crystal scars, umbral wastes) ~40-46 (up to 50) — past that, bring a party and a will (dungeons). Each highland's lv-70 ancient wears its weather as a warning ring: if the air itself starts hurting, turn back or press on.

🌓 A full day lasts 24 minutes — the clock sits top-center. Evenings run orange, nights dark blue. The undead (skeletons, wraiths) rise only at night or in dark places (dungeons, deep-forest gloom); dire wolves den only in forests; goblins and slimes roam forests, plains and dungeons. 🔥 Torches push back the dark: bind one to a hotbar slot, press its key, then click a nearby spot to plant it — it lights the ground around it until you pick it back up with E. Whittle spares from 🪵 wood (Inventory → Crafting).

🏹 Bows are real bows now: equip one, bind arrows to hotbar slots (1–9) in 🎒 Inventory, and fire — arrows are spent in slot order, so the earliest slot's stack goes first. Venom/cinder/frost arrows poison, burn and freeze.

☠️ Status cuts both ways: poison (% max HP/s), burn (flat dmg/s + lowers END), freeze (can't move/act), paralyze (lowers AGI+STR), blindness (short sight; your screen edges blacken). Cure with Cleanse/Panacea spells, cures (Inventory → 🔨 Crafting), curative herbs, or kind clerics. 🪓 Trees fall to your blade — wood fuels bombs and arrows. 🌿 Wild herbs glimmer in forests and meadows: press E to gather them (they regrow in a few minutes) — chew raw for +15% HP, or gather sage/cinder/calm/bright herbs to cure poison/burn/paralyze/blindness, or brew them into cures (Inventory → Crafting). Herbalists sell curative herbs by the armful. 🎒 Bind potions, bombs, arrows and gear to hotkeys 1-9 from the inventory.

📕 New spells come only from spellbooks in chests and dungeons. Level up for stat points (💪🛡️🤸🧠😊🍀) and skill points to master spells (potency / efficiency / mastery).

💬 Every NPC remembers you — and every NPC carries their own gold and goods. Press ⚖️ Trade in chat (or type buy potion / sell herb), haggle with offer 20 for potion — or just offer 20, how about 18?, cheaper? once something's under discussion (do you want a potion for 15 gold? and ok take it work too) — seal it with deal in chat or the Deal 🤝 button. They can only sell what they carry and only pay what they hold — and every 10 minutes their shelves restock and their purses refill, with goods matched to their trade (herbalists overflow with 🌿 herbs and cures, hunters with arrows, smiths with gear). Chat raises 💛 loyalty; at 20+ you can recruit (up to 4). Party members fight and level with you.

⚔️ It's a living battlefield: monsters hunt villagers (and sometimes each other!), and anyone you hit — villager, guard or goblin — fights back with their own spells, weapons and potions. Guards step in to defend. The slain leave corpses: press E to raid them for gold and drops (robbing townsfolk in front of witnesses has consequences!). Learn ✨ Revive from spellbooks (priests and high-level healers sell the tome) to raise any corpse — but only if your level is at least its level. Monsters run lv 1 near Emberhold and deadlier further out and deeper down. Healers sell heals, chests hide gold, bosses hide in crypts. 💚 Party members with mending kits patch you up when you're hurt or afflicted — and passing clerics — healers, priests and clerics (teal/blue hoods) — mend and cleanse you as you walk by. Party friends with Heal/Cleanse/Panacea watch over you too. Learn ✨ Panacea or single cures to return the favor.
