# Emberhold — Living World Dungeon Crawler

Infinite 2D action RPG with a programmatically woven living world (Prexzy-powered NPC chats).

## How it works   
- Walk anywhere. Every 16x16 chunk of the world gets a **global coordinate** and is **stored** (custom LocalKv store over IndexedDB, auto-migrated from kv-plugin), so the town, forest, lake or dungeon you found is still there tomorrow. Out in the wilds you'll find **villages, towns, walled cities, canal watergates, ancient sakura towns, dwarven fortresses, desert souks, steppe ger camps, mesa pueblos, frost holds — plus far-off trade cities, port cities with harbors and lighthouses, and a double-walled capital with a castle keep, palace and authorities** — each with its own look, names and townsfolk.
- Base terrain generates instantly (deterministic), then the **world director** (`src/world/ai_director.js`) weaves the rest programmatically: place names (culture pools + C/V phonotactics), lore (swappable per-context lines in `src/world/lore_pool.js`), who lives there, what lurks there — and writes it into the stored chunk.
- **NPCs** are generated as you explore. Each has a home tile, stats, abilities, personal gold/goods, a unique **persona** (temperament, talking style and hobbies shaped by name, profession, weapon and wares — plus one odd hobby each) and **their own memories**. Talk with **E** — every NPC chats (Prexzy chat service for the reply text, with a persona-flavored offline fallback) and remembers you. Names are woven programmatically per culture (Japanese rendaku compounds for sakura, Norse-dwarven holds, nautical ports, pastoral English towns) plus a generic consonant/vowel phonotactic weaver for collisions; repeats in one place get numbered ("… 2"). **Regions** reuse their settlement's center-seeded name; taken names re-roll the phonotactic weaver with a salted seed until the name is new. Uniqueness scans the whole save slot (stored + loaded) and slots never cross-count — deleted worlds are gone for good.
- Fight **mobs** (slimes, goblins, skeletons, wraiths, wyverns, raptors, dungeon bosses). You, NPCs and mobs all have **HP/MP/ATK/DEF/SPD, XP, levels and abilities** (fireball, heal, whirlwind, water_jet, smite, tideburst...). Melee is weapon-based for everyone — your 🔪 Rusted Sword (the default weapon) does the swinging, and every NPC wields an actual blade too, no Slash spell anywhere. Daggers are fast/short, spears reach far, swords/axes/hammers share reach as blade/pierce/blunt; whips trade damage for long reach, wide sweeping cracks and stunning lashes (and the taming arts answer only to a whip); silver and dawnstone bite the unholy — undead and demons alike (5x, and dawnstone garb wards its wearer 30% against them); wands cut spell mana and staves boost power (first bound one in hotbar order counts); elemental rings (fire/water/ice/electric/holy/strength) add 1.5x; light/heavy armor blunts physical hits, mage robes blunt spells — and every armor adds explicit magical defence (SDEF) by tier, while six shields (wooden → dragonbone) guard passively (+DEF/+SDEF) and parry blows at 1/20 x tier.

## Controls
- WASD / arrows: move. Mouse: face. Attack button / click: uses your default weapon (pick it in 🎒 Inventory via ⚔️ Default weapon, or press any weapon hotkey in-world; chops trees when no foe is near). 🔥 Torches: bind to a hotbar slot, press its key, then click a nearby spot to plant it (E collects it back). 1-9 + Space: hotbar (spells + bound items; empty Space = attack). Q: use your off-hand slot (pick it in 🎒 Inventory — spells/throwables fire, weapons/shields/empty parry: 1/5 fists, 1/10 weapon, 1/20 x shield tier). J: quests. E: talk / loot / enter dungeon. R: ride / dismount. M or click minimap: world map (drag pan, wheel zoom). P: party. I: inventory. C: character (spend points). V or click profile panel: full profile card. **Esc: pause menu** (save, load slots, sound, quit to title).
- Mobile: left joystick to move, right-side buttons to attack (big ⚔️) / off-hand (🛡️) / interact (**E**), hotbar to cast, ☰ button for the menu. The joystick + attack + interact buttons auto-hide on non-touch devices; override anytime in ☰ → 🎮 Controls.
- Tutorial prompts: entering a fresh world offers 🎓 Wayfarer Training once the hearth intro is resolved (first main quest done + oath charm chosen or refused) — global, all worlds, default ON. Toggle it via the ⚙️ Settings button / ⚙️ Options sidebar on the title screen or ☰ → 🎓 Tutorial in the pause menu (saved in localStorage `emberhold-tutorial-v1`). Manual entry points (❓ Help → 🎓 Tutorial, ☰ → 🎓 Training) always work even when prompts are off or the intro is still open.
- HUD: top single-line notification banner (fades in/out in place, never stacks, never scrolls); 🌓 day/night clock **top-center** (12-hour time with AM/PM — click it to switch to 24-hour; a full day lasts 24 minutes — evenings orange, nights dark blue); top-left column (☰ menu, 💾 save, 🔔 notifications (N), ❓ help + 📍 location name on top — click it, or M, for the full map; minimap pinned below at the corner with a ⤡ drag corner to resize, 84–320px, remembered; the minimap repaints live at 15Hz on its own loop timer, independent of HUD refreshes); top-right reference-style player panel (collapsible via its ▾/▸ header, expanded by default, remembered; portrait, name, class, Lv/XP, glowing available-points row, HP, MANA, gold, party) with full-name ⚔️ Party / 🏆 Achievements / 🎒 Inventory buttons — click the panel body (or V) for the full profile card; click its points row (or C) to spend.
- Level-up: +3 stat points (STR/END/AGI/INT/CHA/LUK); skill points are earned per tree by practice instead of levels — hits feed weapon trees (25 hits/point), casts feed spell/technique trees (15/10 per point), parries feed the guard trees (6 per point), armor/robe hits taken feed Stoneskin/Wardweave (12 per point), physical hits dealt feed Ascetic in kasaya (25 per point), spell hits dealt feed Devotion in a habitus (15 per point), points never cross trees and stop once a tree holds enough to max it out (gated weapon trees — swordplay, reaving, sundering, venoms, crackling, phalanx, archery, channeling, overcharge, martial arts, parrying, shieldwork — plus per-spell trees with Potency/Efficiency/Mastery/Surge (active weapon techniques ride the same shape; learnt passives grow their own Might/Opening/Mastery/Apex tree)) — gold banner + steady gold highlight on the HUD points row and 🏆 button point to Character (C); profile card shows the same spend bar. Sword lines hit harder with twin strikes and moonlit waves, axes bleed, hammers sunder armor and stun, whips sting-stun and snap back a double echo. Charm hardens the whole party — party members and tamed beasts gain +5 ATK/MATK/DEF/SDEF per point. New spells come only from spellbooks (chests, dungeons, bosses). Bind anything to 1-9 + Space in Inventory. New games start with a 🔪 Rusted Sword bound to slot 1 (the default weapon — the attack button uses it), 🔥 Fireball in slot 2, and ⏏️ Eject in slot 3.
- Themes: 6 looks (🌲 Forest Vale by default, plus 🔥 Emberhold, 💙 Brawl Blue, 🩸 Crimson Crypt, 🌸 Sakura Dawn, 🌙 Midnight Neon) via the 🎨 dropdown in the title-screen ⚙️ Options sidebar or the pause menu 👁️ Appearance section — HUD panel, all menus, title/loading screens, profile card and banners all follow. Saved in localStorage (`emberhold-theme-v1`), implemented in `src/core/theme.js` as pure CSS `body[data-theme]` overrides.
- Published page: `$meta header mode = minimal` collapses the published site header to a hover button.
- **Bows are real bows**: equip one, bind arrows to hotbar slots (🎒 Inventory) — firing spends arrows in slot order (earliest slot first). Venom/cinder/frost arrows poison, burn and freeze; steel/silver/mythril/dragonbone shafts hit harder (1.3/1.5/1.8/2.2x) — all fletched ×20, one ore per batch. **Thrown arms need no bow**: 💨 smoke bombs blind everyone caught (stock up — one spent per throw), ✴️ shurikens hurl in five tiers (1.0→2.6x, cast ×20 per ore) with their own 🌀 Hurling skill tree, plus 💥 exploding (6x + burn + splash) and 🟢 slime (slow + poison) shuriken techniques.
- **Status effects cut both ways** (`src/core/status.js`): poison (% max HP/s until cured), burn (flat dmg/s + lowers END until cured), freeze (can't move/act), paralyze (lowers AGI+STR until cured), blindness (sight ~3 tiles + a small chance for its hits to miss; a blinded player's screen edges go black, HUD unaffected). Cure with Cleanse/Panacea spells, crafted cures, curative herbs, or kind clerics. Poison/burn kills credit the attacker for XP (tracked via `srcId`). Every live effect shows as a chip with its remaining time on the hero panel, the profile card (V) and each friend's Party tab — buffs (Shieldwall, Warcry, Ember/Blessed Weapon, Unseen, Honed, Roused) in green, debuffs in red.

Herbalists sell them in bulk. **Buildings work too**: beds sleep, church **altars cleanse + heal**, anvils hone blades, war drums rouse, hearths warm, bath pools restore, tea tables pour mana, thrones and feast tables mend, offering bowls bless (10g), rune circles recharge — floating labels say what **E** does. **📖 Lorebooks** gather dust on shelves in chapels, churches, cathedrals, shrines, tide temples, homes, halls and shops — and on delve floors (libraries hold whole stacks): press **E** to read in place. 56 sagas × 4 volumes (224 multi-paragraph chapters, 4+ paragraphs each) across six quarreling faiths — ☀️ Sun Throne, 🌊 Tide Covenant, 💀 Pale Liturgy, 🔥 Ember Creed, 🌿 Rootcircle, 🕳️ Gloam Witnesses — who agree on what happened and call each other liars about what it means (one faith's god is another's utmost evil). Nothing is told straight; piece it together from torn, washed, contradictory parts. Your 📖 modal tracks every fragment found. Quest-givers half-remember these books too: most quest stories are colored indirectly by 1–4 random fragments (a rumor, a torn page, a detail to check — never retold), and very rarely, when you enter a delve whose boss matches a rolled book, the quest names the beast straight from its pages. **Clerics** (healers, priests, clerics) mend and cleanse you as you pass by; party members with Heal/Cleanse/Panacea patch you up mid-fight (only spells they know).

## Files

Load order is `index.html` top-to-bottom (engine compat first, `game.js` before `ui.js`/`codex.js`, net last) — keep it when adding files. Layout:

- `src/core/` — engine compat, save store, config, look/status models, worldgen workers, audio, theme.
- `src/world/` — worldgen, buildings, schedules, entities, items, trade/shops, names, lore, AI director, quests, main story, guild.
- `src/actors/` — overworld bosses + mob packs (`bosses.js`, `mobs.js`, `mobs2.js`).
- `src/sprites/` — pixel-art core + animation + all `sprite_*` override layers (overhaul/realism passes, celestial/hell parity, goddess/peter, ink outline).
- `src/dims/hell/` + `src/dims/heaven/` — one folder per dimension (world hook, mobs, sprites, bosses).
- `src/app/` — `game.js` (loop), `ui.js`, `codex.js`, `boss_data.js` (one-time `src/data/bosses.yaml` loader), `changelog.js` + `CHANGELOG.md`.
- `src/data/` — `bosses.yaml`: every warlord (stats, kits, tiers, themes, bulwark, trims, tricks) + tier bulk. Edit + reload to rebalance; no JS needed.
- `src/net/` — multiplayer (`mp_host.js`, `mp_net.js`, `multiplayer.js`); `src/lib/` is the vendored PeerJS client.
- `src/shots/` — title-screen slideshow images.

- `main.pjs` — `$meta` only (names/roles/kinds are woven by `src/world/names.js`; no static lists).

- `src/sprites/sprites.js` — procedural pixel-art (tiles, actors, portraits). Heroes render through the player path with their `appearance` (skin/hair/eyes/outfit/accessory) + equipped gear; `spriteRefFor` is the canonical look shared by overworld, HUD and chat portraits. Tile variants: grounds/trees/lamps ship 2-4 deterministic variants each (`tileFor`/`tileVariantIndex`, stable across loads, no storage); trees/pines/sakura/lanterns are transparent overlays composited over the nearest ground-like neighbor at bake time, so they sit on any terrain instead of dragging a grass square along; chests are transparent overlays too. Static tiles carry outlines BAKED into the pixels (`bakeOutline`, once at paint time; flowers carry a hand-baked 1px rim, since `bakeOutline` on their opaque tile would ring the tile border) — the runtime vector-ink pass is skipped entirely (the old `sprite_tile_ink.js` runtime pass was retired and removed) to save computation; dynamic mobs/bosses/NPCs/boats keep their runtime masks in `sprite_ink_outline.js`. Water/deep-water are textured static base tiles (4 shallow + 3 deep deterministic variants: soft depth patches plus varied motifs — sine wavelets, comma curls, calm dashes, ripple arcs, glints) with an animated per-frame pass (`drawWaterFX` in `game.js` + `drawWaterMotifs`/`waterFlow` in `sprites.js`): shaded 5-tone wave-strokes (faint halo, broken crest, lit face, wide two-tone shadow foot) scroll steadily eastward like a current (deep water lazier, neighbours async) while crossfading between two motif sets so shapes genuinely change, glints twinkling in place, grain-dithered depth fields in the bake, motifs drawn at 60% opacity so they sit in the water — sprite and motion are the same art, so they always agree — plus pulsing shore foam. Shore shading bakes into the static ground (`drawShoreShadow` in `game.js`): no banks, no outlines — the water takes a two-step inner shadow along every land|water border (darkest at the edge) and the land takes a thin light lip, so shading alone sets the water visibly BELOW the land (concave/inset); bridges render full water tiles with their plank band redrawn over the middle (`drawBridgeWater`) — pixel-identical to the surrounding current, zero seam; submerged divers show a faint body silhouette + bubbles that sit ON the water surface and pop in place instead of any static emoji; nothing mirrors onto water (shore reflections were tried and removed — they read as a smear). The ground bake adds a second static pass (`drawBoundaryEdges` in `game.js`): where two DIFFERENT ground-like terrains meet, the shared border is darkened, while same-type neighbors draw nothing (grass/tallgrass/flowers count as one meadow group, so grass|flower borders stay clean). A second static pass (`drawBuildingEdges`) rims building exteriors the same way: house/stone walls, doors, windows (plus dungeon walls/doors) darken the edges facing non-building tiles, interior seams untouched. IMPORTANT: `bosses.js`/`mobs.js`/`mobs2.js` each wrap `Sprites.actor` for their custom mob kinds — the wrappers MUST forward the trailing `app` AND `gen` arguments (they once dropped app, which silently rendered every hero/NPC in the legacy default look). Gender threads as trailing `gen` (male/female/"") through `actor`/`drawActor`/wrappers/`spriteRefFor`: humanoids get structural dimorphism (female lashes + waist), beasts get the `genderFX` post-pass (males darken, females lighten + shrink ~10%). `drawPlayerArmor` covers every armor id in `src/world/items.js` plus a class-derived fallback (heavy/light/robe from resPhys/resMagic) so new armors can never render as "no armor".

## Tames & Stable (Nov 2026)
Weaken tamable beasts until they submit 💛 (E to tame fast — each beast gives up at its OWN random point below 25% HP, and hitting it again can kill it: overshooting the point kills it outright). Up to your 🐾 Taming alongside cap travels with you (base 1 + Pack Bond ranks, up to 5 — press R to ride/dismount, or Ride one from the Party menu, anywhere); extras rest in the Stable (taming over cap included). Grow the 🐾 Taming tree (Character → 🐾 Taming) for more slots, insect taming (Vermin Lore: tarantulas, scorpions, ants, hornets), most wild animals (Broad Kinship), bigger game (Big Game / Apex Bond), and the 🦗 Locust Swarm technique (50 lifetime tames incl. 10 insects — selling or releasing never un-earns it). Warlords never submit; the lv-70 👑 apex ancients (even the Peak Dragon) submit only for a FULLY maxed Taming tree — and even then only 1 in 20 beaten ancients ever asks. Horses ride fast on land; tide turtles/kelpies swim; cinder steeds and magma tortoises cross molten rock. Sea hunters dive and surface; dungeon lakes hide more ambushers — water fiends in sunken pools, magma eels and cinder maws in lava. Every hearth has a 🐴 Stable — E at its pen nexus to file beasts into bought 6x6 BOX grids (first row of each box free, extra pens + new boxes cost gold with each box dearer than the last), or open the 🐴 Horse Market tab to buy stable-bred horses (1500–9500g: more HP, faster, tougher hides than wild horses, but no attack training) and sell your horses for level-based gold — only horses ever trade. Stables do nothing else: no breeding, no archive. Bought horses count as tamed. A ridden mount dies with its rider, and lent mounts only ever appear under their riders. Open a friend's Party tab → 🐴 Their mount to lend them a beast — they ride it at mount speed and stick with you. Lodges are signposted: floating 🐴 over the pen in view, 🐴 on hearth map labels + pins, Find Stable → compass in the party tab. The lodge modal's 📖 Bestiary tab is the in-game monster compendium — every beast you've met, with stats, abilities and slain counts (unseen kinds show as ??? until encountered). Bigger hearths also keep a 🧬 Beast Lodge (all towns/cities+, ~half of small hamlets) — E at its gene-cradle nexus opens the lodge modal on its 🧬 Breed tab: pick two living same-kin beasts of opposite sexes (one ♂ male + one ♀ female; genderless can't breed) — steeds (any horse × any horse, incl. cinder steeds), hounds (wolves, pups, tanuki, jackals, foxes, gnolls), shellbacks, coils, skyborn, swarms, else same kind (every wild beast rolls its own stats, so same-kind pairs combine their rolls), pay a fee scaled by levels/stats/attacks, and hand them over for a few real minutes (several pairs at once). Return to the same beast lodge to collect both parents + one newborn — a random mix of their stats/attacks, sometimes mutated (±stats and/or a bonus attack) — which counts as tamed for skill points and technique unlocks. Guards, hunters and travelers may ride their own mounts (🐴 nameplates) — they keep them when recruited.

## Inns (retire & retrieve)
- Press **E at any inn counter** to open the inn roster: **retire** party members and slaves into paid rooms (first 2 free, further rooms cost more each time — like stable pens), then **retrieve them from ANY inn** worldwide. ## Multiplayer channels + reincarnate
- ♻️ Reincarnate any slot: same world, fresh lv1 hero with a new name + new look (in-page creator). Rename/restyle anytime via ☰ → ✏️ Name or the profile card ✏️ — same hero, new face, saved everywhere including channels.
- 🌐 Multiplayer channels (Minecraft-style hosting): the creator hosts the world on their own machine. Title Multiplayer tab has 🌍 Created Worlds (your hosted saves — reopen the same link anytime) + 🧭 Visited Worlds (links to friends' worlds — rejoin pings the host first and tells you if they are offline instead of loading an empty world; your hero restores where you left). Every player keeps their own local nicknames for worlds (📛). Ended links stay valid for the host: reopening revives the same world/code. There is no game server: the public PeerJS cloud only introduces your browser to the host's (single-session 12-char key, no world data), then gameplay flows browser-to-browser with the host at the center — direct WebRTC DataChannels where they connect, host relay otherwise — so latency follows your connections. The host simulates the living world for every player's bubble (its own + each joiner's location: chunks, NPCs, mobs, base garrisons) and publishes two-tier snapshots covering them all (full identity+clock ~1.6Hz, fast pos-only ~10Hz delta, merged 20Hz with glide); joiners forward actions (hits) to the host and glide to its state, and take hits back with local dodge/armor/retaliation. When the host saves & exits, the stream ends for everyone with a notice (heroes kept for ping-first rejoin; accidental drops just reconnect). Shared clock, bottom-left chat (live only, no history), trade/give, mutual heals, sleep needs everyone. Remote players glide with interpolation. Remote heroes render pixel-identical to the owner's screen: the pos channel carries compact appearance + equipped weapon/armor ids (4Hz), unequips send an explicit `-` sentinel so stale swords clear instead of lingering, and every armor id has an overlay (class-derived fallback covers future ids). NOTE: every player must load fresh code (hard refresh) — an old cached client still talks to the retired channel server and sees nothing. You SEE other players fight: melee swings, flying spells, cast flashes and bomb blasts replay on your screen (visual-only — wounds still arrive through the damage channels). Direct player chat has no AI moderation.
