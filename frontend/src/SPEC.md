# SPEC — Emberhold Living World RPG
## 1. World model (global coordinates)
- Tile size 32px. Chunk 16x16 tiles.
- Global tile coords `(gx, gy)` integers, can be negative. Chunk `(cx, cy) = floor(gx/16)`.
- Pixel pos = tile*32 + offset. Entity pos stored as float global tile coords (e.g. x=3.5).
- Spawn: a randomly founded hearth at tile (0,0) — random kind per new game (village/town/city/watergate/sakura/fortress/desert_souk/steppe_ger/mesa_pueblo/frost_hold, stored on the player as spawnType/spawnName) with its own generated name. Inter-hearth roads radiate from it; every hearth (incl. spawn) holds a ✨ portal POI linking the visited-hearth fast-travel network (portals + Known-world teleport; delves excluded). E on a hearth portal asks: travel on, or bind it as the respawn point (`player.homeHearth` {name,x,y,dim}; death wakes at its portal via safeTownLanding in the dim it died in — hell falls back to last safe ground, then last ground, then the tower; old/unset saves wake at spawn as before). Deaths audit themselves: `lastDeath` keeps coords, level, DEF/SDEF/maxHP, a stat snapshot and the last 8 landed blows (attacker + level + spell/strike + damage, from the hero's 30-hit incoming log); the death screen lists them and the minimap marks the spot while nearby. Routed multiplayer wounds (t:hurt/phit/dHurt) mitigate on the owner's machine with the hero's real DEF/SDEF (the sender's proxy is statless, so the sent amount is ~raw) and join the same hit log.
- Outlying settlements: regions of 8x8 chunks roll at most one settlement center (~65% of regions), never within 72 tiles of spawn. Types: village (r12, thatch+farms), town (r34), city (r75, wall ring with open avenue gates + avenues), watergate canal-city (r36, moat + wall + 2 canals + bridges), sakura town (r32, sand paths, planted/wild sakura trees, pond, gate posts, lanterns), dwarven fortress (r34, lava moat + stone walls + great hall + forge lanterns), desert souk (r13, adobe + oasis pool + awnings), steppe ger camp (r12, felt gers + horse pens), mesa pueblo (r13, terrace adobe + shaded plazas), frost hold (r13, snow timber + frozen pond + runestones), trade city (r85, far-off only: downgraded to town within 120 of spawn; grand bazaars + counting houses + merchant villas), port city (r80, far-off coastal: downgraded to watergate within 120 of spawn; harbor bay + piers + lighthouse), capital (r95, far-off only: downgraded to town within 140 of spawn; double outer wall + inner citadel wall with the huge village-sized Grand Castle dead-center (throne room + garden, structural like the great hall), plus palace/chancery/high temple). Three area tiers ~10x apart: hamlets (r11-13, ~7 plots, homes+small holy+inn/stable only — no important services), towns (r32-36, ~65-95 plots, full service set + guild), metros (r75-95, ~150-300 plots, full service set + guild, castles capital-only). Metros need empty land around them: a metro roll within both radii + 12 of a nearby settlement downgrades to town, so 75-95-radius walls never swallow their neighbours. Non-metro neighbours can still overlap (rolls are independent — verified live: fortress/fortress 50 apart, sakura/town 48 apart): building placement is neighbour-aware (a lodge is only placed where its own settlement wins every footprint + door-front tile under the bake's nearest-wins rule, and never inside a neighbour's ring/moat/canal/pond/road band), and the tile backfills only stamp tiles their building's settlement owns, so overlap zones stay coherent instead of stamping walls through each other. Port bays (ellipse rx R*.38, ry R*.19, piers every 14 tiles + axes) and the capital inner citadel wall (ring at R*.38) scale with radius in world tiles, placement exclusions and the wall probe alike. Settlement tiles count as town biome (no mob spawns); whole multi-chunk settlements share one center-seeded fallback name; AI enrichment reuses a sibling chunk's enriched name. New tiles: SAKURA (20, solid, choppable for wood), STONEWALL (21, solid), LANTERN (22, walkable decor). Save v10. Stale-bake migration: chunks stored under older versions re-bake on load when they still hold building tiles or fall inside a current settlement (name/lore/enrichment kept) — otherwise pre-scale layouts mismatch the new walls and read as collapsed (bare ground + furniture, no exterior). Heal-first backfill (Game.backfillStableTiles, every 8s on loaded chunks): stamp runs even for buildings that read as collapsed — a wall-less footprint with no RUBBLE/wallHP is stale data, so bare ground/trees heal to the current footprint; player damage (RUBBLE, wallHP, TORCH, POIs, non-tree solids) is never overwritten, so smashed holes and demolished buildings stay as left. The stamp yields to fortifications: ring-band and enemy-holding ring tiles are never overwritten (no placement ever claims them; ghost-clear already skipped them). All three tile backfills invalidate the ground cache when they dirty a chunk, so healed tiles paint immediately. Session-cache hardening: World mem maps are LRU-capped (chunks 600, npcs 1200, dungeons 80, enriched queue 5000) and mob paint caches are LRU-capped at 400 — all are pure write-through caches, so eviction only re-reads from storage.
- Enemy holdings: a separate 6x6-chunk region grid rolls at most one holding (~28% of regions) with HARD placement — never within 150 tiles of spawn, never on water, spaced ≥70 from other holdings and 60+ past every settlement's radius. EIGHTEEN types — twelve surface (weighted: greenskins/brigands/kobolds/gnolls common, deep horrors rare) plus six hell-only (even muster): goblin village (r14, palisade + water moat), orc stronghold (r16, DOUBLE-thick stone ring + lava moat), brigand camp (r13, palisade, no moat), kobold warren (r11, shored burrows + scrap works), gnoll den (r13, bone-hide tents), skeleton crypt (r14, stone, bone chapels + ossuary), cultist sanctum (r14, stone + lava moat, dark cells + rite circles + pit chapel), haunted barrow (r13, palisade + bog-water moat, mounds + spirit shrines), minotaur maze (r16, DOUBLE-thick stone labyrinth + bronze forge + bull hall), serpent temple (r15, stone + sunken-water moat, coil dens + tide chapel + egg vault), harpy roost (r13, nest platforms + rookeries + matriarch's perch), troll den (r14, stone hovels + mossy feast hall); hell: cinder warren (r14, stone + lava moat), gore hold (r13, stone), obsidian citadel (r16, DOUBLE-thick stone + lava moat), flesh pit (r14, palisade), marrow ossuary (r15, stone), gloomspire (r14, DOUBLE-thick stone). The continent decides the roster — hell ground never rolls a surface holding and the surface never rolls a hell one. Gates cut every ring on both axes. Each holds its own signature building classes (own palette + furn kits — no stable, no lodge), a war-sign with its deterministic name, and a master chest on walkable ground near the middle. Center chunk records `base:{type,cx,cy}` (skull-marked on the world map, never a hearth-travel stop); chunks whose center falls inside muster no villagers. Holdings pave only wild tiles (never spawn ground or settlements); generic wild spawns skip their chunks — the garrison system covers them. Fort data (wall/thick/moat) lives in BASE_DEF and drives tiles, ring-HP thickness and reach alike, so new types plug in with data only.
- Biome at tile: deterministic value-noise + distance rules, each frontier biome gated on its own noise blob so regions stay separate:
  - d<20 town, 20..64 mixed forest/plains/lake, >64 wilder (denser forest, more water, dungeon entrances).
  - d>100 seas (open water, sandy islets), d>130 deserts (dunes, oasis pools, palms, sandmaws), d>150 tundra (ice sheets, snow pines), d>170 oceans (deep water, rare islets), d>210 mountains (pines, rock slabs, alpine tarns, yetis), d>260 volcanoes (ashfields, lava seeps, ember vents, phoenixes), d>270 frostcrown glaciers (ice sheets, snow pines, frost-spires), d>285 blightmire bogs (black pools, bone-white dead, moss), d>300 stormcrag cliffs (storm spires, rock slabs, sky-tarns), d>320 crystal scars (singing crystal clusters, ash, sand), d>340 umbral wastes (ash, bone fields, void shards) — each rarer and deadlier than the last, and every highland blob runs wide on purpose. d>90 dark forests (moss, wall-to-wall canopy, gloomspinners) where vegetation+gloom noise run high. No settlements in the open ocean (ports on sea-coasts are fine — town tiles pave over shore).
  - Mob curve per biome (overworld never passes 50 — only lv-70 apex ancients in the seven highlands do): town/vale bell ~3, village outskirts ~5, forest 8–15 (deep 14–26), dark forest 14–26, desert 13–28, tundra 15–30, mountain/frostcrown 24–50, blightmire 26–50, volcano/stormcrag/crystal scar 28–50, umbral waste 30–50, sea 10–24, ocean 14–30, else the old distance ramp (≤12). Grave hunger: landed direct hits (never status ticks) on non-boss undead mobs (role mob, not tames/party/clones — isUndead incl. every undead-flagged template) roll 10% to spawn the same kind at ±1 level adjacent via makeMob + walkable-spiral, aggroed on the attacker (dungeon splits inherit dungeonId, MP ownerHash stamped). Summoned splits are ordinary mobs, so they split too — unbounded by design. Bosses (isBossEnt) exempt. Enemy holdings muster their own breed tables instead — goblin/brute/shaman + Goblin King; orc grunt/shaman + Orc Warchief; bandit/outlaw/murderer + Bandit Warlord; kobold/shaman + Kobold King; gnoll/hunter + Gnoll Alpha; skeleton/draugr/gasha + Skeleton Warlord; cultist/striga/utukku + Hierophant of the Pit; wraith/banshee/herald + Wraith Sovereign; minotaur calf/satyr/foal + Minotaur Warlord; naga/gorgon/hydra spawn + Nāga Rājā; harpy/siren/stymphalian + Harpy Matriarch; troll whelp/leshy/nurikabe + Troll King — at bell(24,4,10,35) with a lv30–35 warlord; EVERY surface holding mob caps at 35, bosses included. Hell holdings muster their own fiends — cinder imp/ash hound/ember crawler/crag fiend/ash mother + Ash Tyrant; gore hound/sanguine horror/exsanguinator/blood monk/bleed mother + Gore Patriarch; obsidian shard/glass maw/mirror fiend/shard ravager/obsidian golem + Gloom Sovereign; flesh mound/maw walker/bile spitter/flesh oracle/muscle brute + Maw Primogenitor; marrow eater/ossuary crawler/skull swarm/bone collector/ribcage walker/ivory tyrantkin + Marrow Sovereign; mirror fiend/razor choir/chalk wraith/obsidian prophet/vitrifier + Eventide Horror — at bell(70,5,60,80) with a lv74–80 warlord (cap 80). Each family's heaviest hitters muster ONLY in their holding's garrison (cut from the wild pools) — the wilds keep the weaker fiends. Garrison-mates are allies (isAlly on shared baseId): no targeting, no melee/AoE/splash damage, no retaliation between holding-mates — infighting only ever picks wild outsiders. War parties march on surface holdings only. Per-biome kind pools incl. phoenix (volcano), yeti (mountain), rime wolves/shiver owls (tundra + frostcrown + stormcrag), gloomspinners/umbral stags (dark forest, umbral waste), sandmaws (desert), coral crabs (sea), abyss serpents (ocean), mire lurkers/nagas/hydra spawns (blightmire), harpies/stymphalians (stormcrag), tomb scorpions/siege ants (crystal scar), wraiths/banshees (umbral waste).
  - Dungeon entrance: chunk hash picks ~1 per 25 chunks when dist>40. Dungeon `depth = 1+floor(entranceDist/120)` (backfilled on old saves): depth 1 is a starter crypt, depth 8+ (700+ tiles out) is ancient ground.
  - Apex lairs (v165): each highland rolls its lv-70 signature ancient on ~0.4% of spawns (65% signature, else rare wildlife); every ancient carries its lair aura (r18 warning ring: slow max-HP status drain + outward shove + vision haze + entry toast). v15 worldgen (tiered services: important shops/guilds only in MID+LARGE towns/cities, castles only in the capital) rebuilds pre-v15 saves once: terrain wiped (chunks + delves), hero kept.
- Chunk record stored in LocalKv `wchunks`, key `c_cx_cy`: `{cx,cy,biome,placeName,lore,tilesBase64 or array,enriched,pois[]}`.
  - tiles: 256 ints. pois: chests, signs, dungeon entrances, fixed NPC spawn slots.
- Dungeon interiors: bounded 104–136-tile maps (seeded 104/116/128 +2/floor, cap 136, god-delve 132, never infinite) in one of five rule-driven layouts — crypt tomb-districts (22-32 halls, L-shaped unions, aisles), winding caverns (sticky cellular caves + stamped halls, winding connectors), maze lattice with area-scaled braiding + treasure nooks, great halls (vast courts on 3-wide boulevards + side crypts), sunken lake (2-4 hazard lakes ringed by edge + interior rooms, crossed by 2-3-wide bridges) — picked by delve seed + floor with theme-native forcing (libraries/storm = halls, clockwork = maze, grove/hive/briar = caverns, tomb/mirror/flesh = crypts; wreck/flesh flood as lakes), with a per-delve theme (20: classic six shifting one step per floor down; `glacier`/`library`/`briar`/`hive`/`wreck`/`leviathan` unlock at 5+, `inferno`/`abyss`/`mirror`/`storm`/`tomb`/`clockwork`/`flesh`/`grove`/`leviathan` at 8+; `leviathan` always bakes the sunken-lake layout and its boss is the diving Leviathan on floors 13+ (shallower leviathan-theme floors wake lesser water lords of the floor's tier instead), which breeds abyss-tentacle adds in nearby water ~every 10s, lashes a random nearby foe ~every 5s while surfaced, and drowns a large disc of the floor around itself ~every 30s to dive under it; leviathan + broodmother boss rooms always bake extra pools, with a pool under the boss itself) driving decor AND set pieces: ice sheets + frozen pools (walkable, slippery — the hero glides), lava lakes with guaranteed black-stone spines, ponds with plank causeways, moss/spore clusters, bone pits + sarcophagi + collapse rubble, underground streams, crystal clusters (solid glow-stone, chamber-only, spaced so they never seal), library shelf stacks + scrolls, briar thorn-trunks, wax comb + propolis, ship decks + bilge pools, mirror vanities, storm lightning-pillars, royal tomb sarcophagi, clockwork gear-grids, blood lakes, deepcap stalks). Walkable floor variants (ice/ash/moss/bone/wax/flesh) + solid crystal and solid blood-hazard tiles, all with art + minimap colors. Detail scales with depth (decor count, pool/lake counts, torch grids, sarcophagi/rubble, crystals; ancient crystal detail + a sealed deep-vault chest past level 8). Districts link as a braided graph (chain + branchiness loops + long shortcuts, winding connectors by floor roll; isolated wilds sealed to rock), entry = first hall, boss = farthest hall BY WALK, portal = boss-room corner nudged onto real floor, vault cache in the second-farthest room. Level keys `d_<cx>_<cy>` (level 1, legacy 40×40) and `d_<cx>_<cy>_L2`, `_L3`, … in kv `wdungeons`: `{id,baseId,cx,cy,level,name,lore,theme,mode,w,h,tiles[],rooms,entryPos,bossPos,portalPos,chests,entrance:{gx,gy},bossId,lore}`. Entering stairs teleports player to dungeon map (level 1); each floor's boss-room corner holds a 🌀 portal: E opens a choice — descend to the next generated floor or return to the surface (entry stairs always climb out). `player.dungeonLevel` persists (default 1, reset to 1 on surface/death). Effective depth = delve `depth` + `dungeonLevel` − 1: mob levels (`mobLevelFor`/`bossLevelFor` tables), chest gold (+35%/depth), corpse purses (+30%/depth, bosses +50%/depth), chest tiers, boss drops and legend caches all scale with it, so every floor hits harder and pays more. Deeper floors inherit the delve's AI name/lore; only level 1 is AI-enriched. Dungeon coords are local to dungeon + recorded as global-ish `dungeonId:x,y`. Shifting delves (game.js `dungeonShiftTick`): below effective depth 3 the walls only breathe (slow open/seal swaps, no damage); from depth 3 every theme fights back with telegraphed hazards that scale with depth (faster, wider, double events at 8+) — water delves (flooded/wreck/leviathan/glacier) surge and recede liquid across the floor, shove everyone and tug the wet with a veering prevailing current; caverns drop rocks, tear the ground to rubble and open walls; ember/inferno well up magma; glacier spreads ice, melts pools and blows gusts; flesh wells blood; crypts slam sarcophagi and jut bone; fungal/grove/briar burst spores and grow walls; shadow blinds and grabs; abyss/mirror grow crystal and tear voids; libraries slide shelves and choke dust; hives rain honey and harden wax; storms arc lightning and howl gales; tombs pour sand and curse; clockwork slides brass walls and slams pistons. All shifts are temporary (auto-revert), shield entry/boss/portal/unopened-chest tiles, never seal occupied tiles, and anyone stranded in surge liquid is put ashore — divers dodge every blast by staying submerged. Dungeon mouths are per-theme canvas sprites (ruined tower, cave mouth, mushroom ring, scorched vent, sunken stair, dark arch, ice arch, lava vent, void rift, sunken door, thorn arch, hive mound, broken hull, crystal gate, storm spire, desert tomb, gear vault, fleshy maw, hollow trunk, whirlpool; wet tiles show whirlpool/sunken stair instead), resolved dynamically from the delve id so old saves need no migration, and the E hint names the mouth. Drowned chunks (no dry land near center) hide water delves (`d_<cx>_<cy>_w`, always leviathan at every level) behind a whirlpool mouth on open water — raft/swim up + E to descend; the whirlpool grips slow waterborne heroes (full sail mostly escapes) with a direct drift + swirl particles. Clear-quest targeting uses the chunk's real dungeon POI id/coords, so water delves credit correctly.

- Dungeon floor travel (v100; bottoms added later): each hero charts depths per delve (`player.dungeonFloors[baseId]` = deepest level reached, recorded on every arrival). Delves END: the god-delve at level 5 (Peter's floor), ordinary delves at a deterministic 12-15 per delve (`Game.delveEndLevel`, stable string hash). Revisiting a delve mouth opens a floor-select menu (levels 1..charted, never past the bottom); the boss-room portal menu offers every charted floor + one push deeper (charted+1, uncharted dark, hidden once the bottom is charted) + the surface; the entry-stairs menu offers the surface + every charted floor. `travelDungeonTo` clamps to charted+1 per hero AND to the bottom, so multiplayer travelers can only visit floors they earned (delve geometry is deterministic per seed, so co-delvers still share one layout). Hell Fragments come from the bottom, not kills: stepping through the FINAL floor's portal to the surface (no boss kill needed) claims the delve's one distinct shard (`Hell.claimDelveFragment`; hell delves never pay; stairs/Eject/death exits pay nothing). Old saves migrate in `migratePlayer`: charted depths truncate to the bottom, heroes saved past it return to the entrance (level 1). New depths toast ("📜 Depth N charted — return any time").
- ⏏️ Eject (v100): every hero knows it (no tome, 0 MP, 25s cooldown, `kind:"eject"`) — flings the caster to the mouse-aimed tile (nearest open ground to it), or 2.5–6 tiles to random walkable ground with no aim; no line-of-sight check either way, so it escapes sealed stone. The shift-tick stuck-rescue also pops wall-embedded entities (previously liquids only) onto `dungeonNearWalkable` with a toast.
- Dragonbone depth rule (v100): non-boss dragon-kind mobs and dragon bosses in delves drop bone/horn only at effective depth 6+ (overworld dragons still pay; delve bosses already spawn-gated at 6+).

## 1c. Heaven dimension
- `p.dim` in {'surf','hell','heaven'} (persisted) + `p.posByDim`; `World` activeDim mirrors it (`getDim` returns all three; chunk keys `v_`, folders `_heaven`). Same coordinate numbers bake separate ground per dim (parallel universes): heaven fills walkable cloud inside the disc (R3500 around 0,0) with lakes/reservoirs as water blobs + grass+tree islands (meadows/groves), pantheon core reads as town; outside the disc bakes impassable `heaven_void`.
